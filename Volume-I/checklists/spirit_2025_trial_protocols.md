# D.5 SPIRIT 2025: Trial Protocols

From Appendix D of *Modern Biostatistics for the Health Sciences, Volume I*.

Use when writing a randomized-trial protocol before recruitment begins. SPIRIT 2025 supersedes SPIRIT 2013 and provides a 34-item checklist plus an explanation-and-elaboration document. Download the authoritative current materials from consort-spirit.org. The domains below are an orientation, not official checklist wording.

The protocol carries a descriptive title identifying the design, population, interventions, and acronym where one exists.

The trial registration number and registry are recorded, along with the protocol version number and its date.

Funding sources, the role of the sponsor, and the responsibilities of each oversight committee are named.

The background states the rationale, summarizing relevant studies and justifying the choice of each comparator.

Specific objectives and hypotheses are stated, with one clearly identified primary outcome.

The trial design is described: allocation ratio, framework such as superiority or non-inferiority, and any adaptive features.

Eligibility criteria, the study setting, and the criteria for study centers and personnel are specified.

Interventions in each arm are described in replicable detail, including criteria for modifying or discontinuing them.

Outcomes are defined with the measurement variable, the analysis metric, the aggregation method, and the time point.

A participant timeline shows enrollment, allocation, interventions, assessments, and visits in schedule form.

The sample size is justified with every assumption and the software stated, following the five elements in Chapter 12.

Sequence generation, allocation concealment, implementation, and blinding are each described separately.

Data collection, management, and monitoring plans are described, including the data monitoring committee and interim analyses.

Plans for consent, confidentiality, ancillary care, dissemination, data access, and protocol amendments are all stated.
