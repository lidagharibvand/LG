# D.4 SRQR and COREQ: Qualitative Research

From Appendix D of *Modern Biostatistics for the Health Sciences, Volume I*.

Use when reporting qualitative work: SRQR covers qualitative studies broadly and COREQ covers interview and focus-group studies specifically; both are indexed and maintained through the EQUATOR Network at equator-network.org, from which the authoritative version named by your journal should be downloaded, the domains below are our summary.

The title and abstract identify the study as qualitative and name the approach or tradition that guided it.

The research question, the tradition chosen, and the reason that tradition fits the question are all stated.

The researchers are described: credentials, occupation, training, and any relationship to participants before the study.

Reflexivity is addressed, the assumptions, prior experience, and potential influence the researchers brought to the data.

The sampling strategy is named and justified, with how participants were approached and how many declined or withdrew.

The setting of data collection is described, including who else was present during interviews or observations.

Data-collection methods are described: the interview guide, whether it was piloted, repeat contacts, and recording.

Duration of interviews or observations, field-note practice, and whether saturation was considered are all reported.

The number of coders, how the coding tree was derived, and whether analysis software was used are stated.

Whether themes were derived inductively, deductively, or both is made explicit rather than left implicit.

Member checking, peer debriefing, triangulation, or other credibility procedures are described along with their results.

Findings are presented with sufficient participant quotations, each identified so the reader can trace its source.

Quotations are consistent with the findings they illustrate, and disconfirming or minority accounts are reported.

Limitations, transferability, ethics approval, conflicts of interest, and funding are all stated.
