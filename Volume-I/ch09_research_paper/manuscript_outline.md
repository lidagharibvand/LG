# Manuscript outline

The section order of Chapter 9, as a file to write into. Each heading carries
the question that section has to answer. Delete the questions as you replace
them with text.

## Title
Does it name the design, the population and the outcome, without a colon-joined
clause that says nothing?

## Abstract
Background, methods, results with numbers, conclusion. Does the conclusion
follow from the numbers that are actually in the abstract?

## Introduction
What is known, what is not, and what this study adds. Three paragraphs is
usually enough. Does the last sentence state the objective?

## Methods
Design, setting, participants, variables, data sources, bias, sample size,
statistical methods. Could somebody repeat the study from this alone?

## Results
Participants, descriptive data, outcome data, main results, other analyses.
Are effect sizes given with confidence intervals rather than p-values alone?

## Discussion
Key results, limitations, interpretation, generalizability. Are the limitations
the real ones, or the ones that are safe to admit?

## Other information
Registration, protocol, funding, conflicts, data availability.
